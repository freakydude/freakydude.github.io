Personal data is processed only by the hosting provider and by the few third-party services listed below.

### 2. Hosting by GitHub Pages

This blog is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA.

When you open a page, your browser sends technical data to GitHub's servers, in particular your IP address, the date and time of the request, the requested page, the referrer URL, and your browser and operating system. According to GitHub, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes. I have no access to these logs.

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. My legitimate interest lies in providing the website securely and reliably. GitHub, Inc. is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, on which the transfer to the USA is based (Art. 45 GDPR).

More information: <https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement>

### 3. Storage in your browser

The site stores two settings in your browser's local storage: your choice of light or dark theme (`pref-theme`) and the scroll position of the menu (`menu-scroll-position`). This data never leaves your device and is not sent to me or anyone else. You can delete it at any time in your browser settings.

Storing these settings is strictly necessary to provide the function you requested (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG). The legal basis for any related processing is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR.

### 4. Comments with utterances

Blog posts include a comment section provided by the open-source service utterances (<https://utteranc.es>). Comments are stored as issues in the public GitHub repository of this blog.

When you open a blog post, your browser loads the comment widget from utteranc.es and the existing comments from GitHub. In doing so, your IP address and the address of the page you are viewing are transmitted to utteranc.es and GitHub.

To write a comment, you sign in with your GitHub account and authorize the utterances app. Your comment is then published together with your GitHub username and profile picture, and it is processed by GitHub under its own privacy statement. utterances keeps the sign-in token in your browser's local storage. You can edit or delete your comments on GitHub at any time.

The legal basis for loading the comment section is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; my legitimate interest lies in enabling discussion about the posts. Signing in and commenting is your own choice (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR), and you can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future.

More information: <https://github.com/utterance/utterances> and the GitHub privacy statement linked above.

### 5. Links to other websites

The blog contains plain links to other websites, for example to GitHub, Discord, YouTube, Buy Me a Coffee, Ko-fi (including the Ko-fi button below each post, whose image is served from this site), and the share buttons below each post. No data is transmitted to these services until you click such a link. After that, the privacy policy of the respective provider applies.

### 6. Contact by email

If you contact me by email, I process your email address and the content of your message to answer your request. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, or Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR if your request relates to a contract. I delete the data once your request has been dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

### 7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right

- to access the personal data concerning you (Art. 15 GDPR),
- to rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR),
- to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR),
- to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
- to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR),
- to withdraw any consent you have given, with effect for the future (Art. 7 (3) GDPR).

**You have the right to object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (Art. 21 GDPR).**

To exercise these rights, an informal email to the address above is enough.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority responsible for me is the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany, <https://www.lda.bayern.de>.

I update this privacy policy when the blog or the services it uses change. The current version is always available on this page.
